# Blessed Friday launch email: how to send it (for Asad)
**Send:** today, or tomorrow (Sunday) at 10:00 am. Sunday morning gets the best email opens in Pakistan.

**Subject (pick one):**
1. Our way of saying shukriya: up to 35% off leather bags
2. The Blessed Friday Sale is here (until Monday 30 Nov)
3. A thank-you before winter: up to 35% off our bags

**Preview text:** Up to 35% off leather bags, until Monday 30 November. Prices already marked down. No code needed.

## Building it in Shopify Email
1. Go to Shopify admin → Marketing → Create campaign → Shopify Email.
2. **If your editor has a "Custom code" / HTML section:** paste the whole file `Blessed_Friday_Launch_Email.html` into it.
3. **If it doesn't:** start from a blank template and rebuild it top to bottom from `Blessed_Friday_Launch_Email_preview.png`:
   - **Header:** your logo on an Espresso (`#241C16`) background.
   - **Image:** `kv-bf-hero-desktop` (already in Files).
   - **Text block** (Espresso background, cream text):
     - Line 1: "BLESSED FRIDAY SALE · NOW ON"
     - Heading: "Up to 35% off our leather bags."
     - Paragraph: the "This has been a year of blessings…" text.
   - **Button:** "SHOP THE BLESSED FRIDAY SALE →", colour `#A0623A`, linking to `kordovanleather.com/collections/sale`.
   - **Product block, 2 per row, 8 products:**
     - Cabin Trolley
     - Forester
     - The Corporate
     - DBLP
     - Gridle
     - Venture Pack
     - Diva Tote
     - Clifford Rustic Brown

     Shopify fills in the sale price and the struck-through old price itself.
   - **Button:** "SEE ALL 26 PIECES".
   - **Text block:** Cash on delivery · Free delivery over Rs 5,500 · Handcrafted in Pakistan.
   - **Text block** (dark): the jackets section, "order by 30 November".
   - **Closing line:** "May this season bring you warmth…  With gratitude, the Kordovan family".
4. **Fonts:** Georgia for headings, Arial for body text.
5. **Before sending:**
   - Send a test to yourself and open it on your phone.
   - Check that every button works and that the prices show with the old price struck through.

Every link carries tracking (`utm_campaign=bf_2026`, `utm_content=launch`), so I can count the orders this email brings in.
