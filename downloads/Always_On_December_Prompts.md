## SKIPPED: "Value graph / cost per year"
I am not making this one, because the numbers aren't defensible yet.
- A cost-per-year chart needs two lifespans: how many years our wallet lasts, and how often a cheap wallet is replaced. We have measured neither.
- Our lifetime craftsmanship promise covers stitching and hardware. It is a repair promise, not a lifespan.
- Any "Rs X per year" figure would therefore be invented, and that breaks our no-invented-data rule.

**What would make it honest (January):** Ahsan collects 3–5 real Kordovan wallets that are still in daily use, from staff or customers who agree. Each one needs a purchase date we can prove from the order record. The ad then says "Rs X per year so far" for those named, photographed pieces.
