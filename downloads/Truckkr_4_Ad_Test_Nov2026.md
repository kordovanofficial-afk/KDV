## Ad 1: THE ZIP (problem → fix)  `tof_truckkr_zip_problem_studio_v1`
**Angle:** loose coins. Most bifolds have no coin pocket; this one zips.
**Hook on image:** "Your wallet has a hole in it. It's called 'no coin pocket'."
**Visual:** the open Truckkr with the outside zip half-open and a few coins and a folded note inside. Beside it, a messy pile of loose coins and a crumpled receipt (the "before").
**GPT prompt:** *Overhead product photo, 4:5, a dark brown crazy horse leather bifold wallet with an external zip pocket half open showing a few Pakistani coins inside, on a dark walnut desk; to the left a messy pile of loose coins and a crumpled receipt; warm window daylight, true-to-life dark brown leather colour (not tan), realistic texture and stitching, no text, no logos.*
**Primary text:** Coins in one pocket, cards in another, cash in a third? The Truckkr puts all three in one slim bifold, with an outside zip so coins stop escaping. Genuine crazy horse leather, handcrafted in Pakistan. Rs 3,800, cash on delivery.
**Headline:** The bifold that zips. Rs 3,800

**Angle:** crazy horse leather scuffs into its own character. Real leather, not rexine.
**Hook on image:** "Scratch it. It only gets better."
**Visual:** a split image. Left: the new wallet, dark and even. Right: the same style after months of use, with lighter scratches, a softer sheen and darker edges. Label it "New" / "After months of use (illustration)".
**GPT prompt:** *Split-screen product photo, 4:5. Left half: brand-new dark brown crazy horse leather bifold wallet, even colour. Right half: the same wallet after a year of daily use, lighter scratch marks, burnished edges, richer patina, slightly softened shape. Same angle and lighting both halves, dark walnut background, photographic realism, no text, no logos.*

## Ad 3: COMPARISON (our proven format)  `tof_truckkr_comparison_value_studio_v1`

**Hook on image:** "Rs 900 wallet vs Rs 3,800 wallet. One year later."
**Visual:** two wallets side by side. Left: a cheap shiny synthetic wallet, peeling at the fold and edges cracked ("Rs 900, rexine, 1 year"). Right: the Truckkr, dark brown, solid, with a light natural patina ("Rs 3,800, crazy horse leather, 1 year"). Right: a dark brown crazy horse genuine leather bifold wallet with an external zip pocket and a light natural patina. Same scale and lighting, realistic, no text, no logos.* (Text and ticks added in Canva.)

**Headline:** Buy once. Rs 3,800
