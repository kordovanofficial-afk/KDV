## 0. HOW TO USE
1. **Upload 2 images first:** ① the real product photo (clean Shopify shot) ② the KORDOVAN logo PNG. Then paste: **LOCK + STYLE + the ad prompt**.
2. Check every output against the real product (buckle, stitching, colour, pockets) and the price table. A wrong bag or price in an ad = a refused COD parcel. Regenerate if anything is off.
3. 🚫 No city names anywhere. 🚫 Diva Tote is **VEGAN**: never "leather" on it.
4. Sizes: feed **4:5 (1080×1350)** · stories/reels **9:16 (1080×1920)** · carousel **1:1 (1080×1080)**. To make a 9:16 version, re-run the same prompt and change the ratio line; text stays in the safe area (not the top 250px or bottom 300px).

### 🔒 LOCK (always first)
```
Use uploaded image 1 as the exact product. Keep it 100% identical: same shape, proportions, stitching, seams, hardware, zips, buckles, embossed logo, colour and leather texture. Do not add, remove or redesign any part of it. Use uploaded image 2 as the brand logo, reproduced exactly, small, never distorted or recoloured except to cream or espresso. Photorealistic commercial photography, full-frame camera, 85mm lens, sharp product focus, natural shadows, true-to-life colour.
```

### 🎨 STYLE (always second): KORDOVAN "Quiet Heritage"
```
Art direction: quiet luxury heritage brand, like a premium menswear editorial, never a cheap sale flyer. Palette only: espresso #241C16, alabaster cream #F7F2EA, cognac #A0623A, aged brass #B0894F, warm stone #8B8174. Warm low-key lighting, rich deep shadows, natural textures (walnut wood, linen, plaster, stone). Typography: headlines in an elegant high-contrast serif (like Fraunces), sentence case, generous letter spacing; small labels in a clean thin sans-serif (like Inter) in uppercase with wide tracking. Text in cream or brass on dark areas, espresso on light areas. Old prices in a thin stone-grey with a fine strikethrough, new prices in cream or brass, larger. Generous empty space, perfectly aligned text, maximum 4 text lines plus the logo. No starbursts, no red, no neon, no exclamation marks, no emojis, no stickers, no 3D text, no drop shadows on type. Spell every word exactly as given, with nothing extra added.
```

### ✍️ Voice rules (already written into the prompts)
Calm and certain, never shouting. Short lines. Talk about the craft and the years ahead, not the "deal of a lifetime". The discount is stated once and plainly. Scarcity is used only when it's true.
