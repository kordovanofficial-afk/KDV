## 0. HOW TO USE (read once)
1. **Always upload the real product photo first** (the clean Shopify photo, best angle) and THEN paste the prompt. The AI builds the scene around OUR product. It must not invent a new bag.
2. **Paste the LOCK block below at the start of every prompt.** It stops the AI changing stitching, hardware, logo or colour.
3. **Never let the AI write text, prices or the logo.** It misspells them. Generate the image clean, then add text, prices and the KORDOVAN logo in Canva.
4. If the output changes the bag (different buckle, extra pocket, wrong colour), **reject it and regenerate.** Customers will compare the ad to what arrives. A mismatch = COD refusal.
5. Make each image in **4:5 (1080×1350)** for feed and **9:16 (1080×1920)** for stories/reels.
6. 🚫 No city names in any creative or caption. Say "handcrafted in our workshop" / "made in Pakistan".
7. 🚫 The **Diva Tote is VEGAN.** Never write "leather" on Diva Tote creatives.

### 🔒 LOCK block (paste at the start of EVERY image prompt)
```
Use the uploaded product photo as the exact product. Keep the product 100% identical: same shape, proportions, stitching, seams, hardware, zips, buckles, logo emboss, colour and leather texture. Do not add, remove or redesign any part of the product. Do not add any text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image. Photorealistic commercial product photography, shot on a full-frame camera, 85mm lens, sharp focus on the product, natural shadows, true-to-life colour.
```

### 🎨 Look and feel (KORDOVAN "Quiet Heritage")
Warm, dark, rich and calm. Espresso browns, cognac, aged brass, warm stone, linen, walnut wood, soft window light or a single warm spotlight. **Never** neon, glitter, red "SALE" bursts or cheap clutter. A premium brand on sale should still look premium. The discount carries the urgency; the image carries the desire.
