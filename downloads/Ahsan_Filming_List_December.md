**For:** Ahsan Khan · **From:** Omar (marketing) · **Start:** Nov 16 · **Send everything by:** the dates below
Send all files on the Kordovan WhatsApp **as a Document** (not as a photo or video), so the quality stays full.

Everything must be **real**: our real workshop, our real products, our real hands. Nothing staged to look like a customer.

## 1. Workshop clips (send by Nov 30)
Make 3 short videos, **10–20 seconds each**:
1. **Cutting:** leather being cut for a wallet (knife or press). Show the leather, the tool and the hands.
2. **Stitching:** a wallet or belt edge being stitched, close up.
3. **Edge finishing:** an edge being sanded, painted or burnished, close up.

How to film:
- Film in **daylight**, near a window or door. No flash, no ring light.
- Vertical is fine; we crop it ourselves.
- Hold the phone steady: lean your elbows on the table, or rest the phone on something.
- Get close to the hands and the leather. Faces are not needed. If a worker's face shows, ask them first.
- No music and no talking needed. Normal workshop sound is good.
- Film each clip 2–3 times, so we can pick the best one.
- Do not say or show the city name or the shop address anywhere.

## 2. Patina before / after (send by Dec 12)
- Find a **real Mocha Mate that someone has used** for a long time. A staff member's wallet, or a customer who agrees, is ideal.
- Put it **beside a brand-new Mocha Mate**: same table, same light, same angle.
- Take 3 photos (front, back, open) and one 10-second video slowly turning both.
- **Write down** whose wallet it is, roughly when it was bought (the order number if there is one) and that they said yes to it being used in an ad.
- **Do not** rub, oil, colour or "age" a wallet to make it look used. It must be really used.

## 3. "Ordered vs arrived" for gloves (send by Dec 20)
- Pack one pair of gloves (black) exactly as we ship them to customers.
- Open the parcel on a plain table in daylight and take 2–3 photos of the gloves coming out of the real box.
- Take the photos as they are. No editing and no filters.

## 4. Three comment-reply ideas (use only if a real person asks)
When someone **really** asks one of these in a comment on our Instagram or Facebook posts, screenshot the comment and film the answer. Hide their name, or ask them first if their name shows.
1. **"Is it real leather?"** Film a close-up of the cut edge of a leather offcut, showing the fibres inside, then the finished wallet next to it. 10–15 seconds.
2. **"Does the coin pocket really hold coins?"** Fill the Mocha Mate coin pocket with coins, close the button, shake it gently and open it again. 10–15 seconds.
3. **"How do the gloves fit?"** Wear an L pair, open and close your hand slowly, and show the wrist. Then show the size chart from the product page. 10–15 seconds.

Send me the screenshot of the real comment together with the video. **Never write a comment yourself to film a reply to it.**

## 5. Photo tips (for everything)
- Use daylight from a window, and stand so the light comes from the side.
- Use a plain background: a wooden table, a cream cloth or a dark cloth. No clutter.
- **Wipe the camera lens** first.
- Make the product fill most of the frame.
- Use the normal camera only. No beauty mode, no filters, no zoom (move closer instead).
- Take more than you think you need: 5–6 angles of each thing.
- Write the product name and colour in the WhatsApp message with each file.

## 6. DM keywords (every day)
When someone messages to order and mentions **MOCHA, GLOVES, BELT or KODO**, log their name, phone number, the keyword and the date in the usual sheet. This shows us which ads bring orders that don't come through the website.

Thank you. These real clips are worth more to us than any designed ad.
