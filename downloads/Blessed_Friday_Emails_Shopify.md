# Blessed Friday Sale: 3 emails for Shopify Email (for Asad)
Build them in **Shopify admin → Marketing → Create campaign → Shopify Email**, using the "Sale" or "Product showcase" template.
- **Send to:** all subscribers.
- **Name:** always "Blessed Friday Sale", never "Black Friday", in the subject and in the body.
- **Colours:** background Alabaster `#F7F2EA`, text Espresso `#241C16`, button Cognac `#A0623A` with cream text.
- **Fonts:** Shopify Email only has web-safe fonts. Use Georgia for headings and Arial for the body.
- **Product blocks:** use Shopify's own product blocks so the price and the strike-through come from the store. Only send emails 2 and 3 AFTER the sale prices are live (Nov 27 00:00).
- **Never write:** "only X left", "hurry before stock runs out", a countdown, Karachi, "our workshop".

---

If there's a bag you've had your eye on, now is a good time to look again.

**Button:** See the bags → `https://kordovanleather.com/collections/kdv-bags`

**Small print:** Cash on delivery all over Pakistan · Free delivery over Rs 5,500 · Wallets, belts, gloves and jackets stay at their normal prices.
