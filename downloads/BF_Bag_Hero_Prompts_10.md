# Blessed Friday: 10 bag hero prompts (for Asad), Nov 21
One prompt per bag. Every image works on its own as a single-image ad **and** as a card in a carousel.

**Make them in this order:** Trolley → Forester → CEO → Branson → Corporate → Executive → DBLP → Venture → Master's → Gridle.

## Rules for every prompt
- Upload the real product photo and the real Kordovan logo PNG.
- Start every prompt with: **"Use the uploaded bag EXACTLY as it is: same shape, panels, stitching, hardware, logo position and colour. Do not redesign it."**
- **Size:** 1080 × 1350 px, 4:5, photorealistic, JPG.
- **Shared text system:**
  - Eyebrow: Inter SemiBold 30 px, letter-spacing +0.2em, Brass `#B0894F`.
  - Headline: Fraunces Bold, Alabaster `#F7F2EA` on dark images, Espresso `#241C16` on light images.
  - Price band at the bottom: 140 px tall, Cognac `#A0623A`.
    - Line 1: sale price, Inter Bold 46 px, Alabaster.
    - Next to it: old price, Inter Regular 30 px, Alabaster at 70%, struck through.
    - Line 2: "Blessed Friday Sale · until Monday 30 November", Inter Regular 26 px, Alabaster at 85%.
  - Logo: 120 px wide, 24 px above the price band, centred, in Alabaster (or Espresso on light images).
- **Never in any image:**
  - coins
  - Karachi
  - "workshop"
  - "only X left"
  - countdown clocks
  - red or yellow sale bursts
  - stars or reviews
  - visible brand logos on laptops or phones
  - faces (hands and bodies cropped at the shoulder are fine)
  - plastic shine
  - extra bags
