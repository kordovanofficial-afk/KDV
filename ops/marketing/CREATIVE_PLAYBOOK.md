# KORDOVAN Creative Playbook: what actually sells (living document)
Started Nov 10 2026 (Founder: "pinpoint the exact formula that sells, not just reach, but revenue").
**Rule:** a format moves up only on **Shopify-confirmed orders** (utm_content), never on Meta's count alone (Meta over-reports ~2.05x). Judge after ≥ PKR 1,000–1,300 spend per ad. Track the **COD refusal rate per ad** too: an ad that sells but gets refused is a loser.

## 5. REJECTED (and why)
- AirDrop / fake system pop-ups: iPhone-only; Meta policy risk.
- Fake Facebook-post / fake chat / fake 1-star review / fake awards: break the no-invented-reviews rule.
- MS Paint lo-fi, FOUND poster, memes: off-brand for Quiet Heritage (revisit only as a deliberate test).
- VIP pre-hype sign-up funnel: products not exclusive enough yet; revisit for an exclusive launch (Founder, Nov 10).

## 6. HOW A FORMAT GRADUATES
Test (10% budget, one new format a week vs the control) → ≥2 Shopify orders at ≤ the category CPA ceiling (wallets ~1,300, gloves 1,000; bags TBD after BF) with a refusal rate ≤ the account average → **PROVEN**, and it joins the always-on new-customer ad set. Two failed tests of the same format with different angles → **LOST**.
