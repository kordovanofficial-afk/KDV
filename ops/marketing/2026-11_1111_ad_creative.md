## 5. Paid use: break-even cost per click (for the CRO, L3)
Formula: break-even CPC = order value × survival × gross margin × checkout rate per session.
- Full price: 2,228 × 0.693 × 0.689 × 0.0166 = **Rs 17.7 per click**. (Margin 68.9% is the CRO's weighted figure for the 8 wallets, from CLAUDE.md Oct 31 costs.)
- With an offer of d: 2,228 × (1 − d) × 0.693 × [(1 − d − 0.311) ÷ (1 − d)] × 0.0166. The CRO fills in d once the Founder approves it.
- Measured CPCs (CLAUDE.md Sep 4 2026): catalogue ads Rs 6.96–8.14 (**under** break-even), static/video Rs 29.69–31.66 (**over**). So for paid, the cheapest route is to feed C1–C4 as **new ads beside the existing Retarget ad** (never edited in place) or to rely on the catalogue format. Not cold static or video. Budget and audience are the CRO's call.

> **Assumptions**
> - A1 sessions per post (content batch A1): Reel 150, carousel/static 100, story day 100. Replaced by `utm_content` reads on Nov 9 and Nov 16.
> - A2 checkout rates per landing page, 90 days to Nov 1, all sources: wallets collection 1.66%, Bastion page 1.83%.
> - A3 wallet order value Rs 2,228 (30-day net ÷ orders across the candidate wallets; content batch A3).
> - A4 survival 69.3% (1 − October reversals 30.7%, data pack).
> - A5 no offer lift assumed. The CRO owns it.

## 6. Production once `cdn.shopify.com` is reachable (free tools only)
1. **Fetch** the §2 URLs into a fresh scratch folder (one directory, nothing executed from it). Request `&width=2160` from the CDN where a larger source exists. Check every file opens and matches its alt text.
2. **Fonts:** Fraunces and Inter from Google Fonts (OFL, free), installed locally.
3. **Statics (C2, C3):** an HTML/CSS layout per card with the brand tokens, rendered to PNG by headless Chromium (free). Or ImageMagick `convert` composites with `-font Fraunces`. Export 1080×1350, 1080×1080 and 1080×1920.
4. **Motion (C1, C4):** either
   - **ffmpeg**: per image `zoompan=z='min(zoom+0.0008,1.06)':d=75:s=1080x1920` on a Parchment `color` pad, `drawtext` for each card, joined with the `concat` demuxer or `xfade=transition=fade:duration=0.2`, `-c:v libx264 -crf 18 -pix_fmt yuv420p -r 30`; or
   - **HyperFrames** HTML-to-video composition (the vendored skill): one HTML scene per frame with CSS transforms for the push-ins, rendered locally.
   No paid generators, and no AI generation or retouching of the product.
5. **Placeholders stay visible** in the review renders as literal `[OFFER]` / `[DATES]` tags in Cognac. The final renders are made only after the Founder approves the offer wording. The CQRO checks the final frame text against the approved recommendation word for word.
6. **Review path:** renders → CQRO (C-checks below) → CEO → Founder. Paid use is a separate L3 approval through the CRO.
