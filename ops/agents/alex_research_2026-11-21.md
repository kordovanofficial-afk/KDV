## Confirmed by multiple sources
- Money Models: 4 offer types — Attraction (strangers → customers), Upsell (spend more), Downsell (turn no into yes), Continuity (keep them). Two stages: get cash, then get more cash. Success test: 30-day gross profit per customer > cost to acquire + serve. Tactics named in summaries: rollover upsell, A/B offers, payment-plan downsell, waived-fee continuity. Upsells often = majority of profit.
- Leads: Core Four = warm outreach, cold outreach, free content, paid ads (warm/cold × 1-to-1/1-to-many). Master one first. Lead magnet = complete solution to a narrow problem that reveals the bigger problem. Rule of 100 (daily volume for 100 days). Lead getters: customers/referrals, employees, agencies, affiliates.
- More → Better → New ordering. LTV/LTGP:CAC floor ~3:1 (higher for human-heavy businesses; sources disagree on exact). LTGP can grow without limit, CAC only to zero. Test new campaigns at ~2× CAC.
- Offers principles apply to e-commerce/physical goods ("same variables, different application"); risk reversal e.g. try-before-charge.
- Ethics themes: "Stand out by telling the truth" (Ep 847); "character and cash flow are inseparable over a prolonged timeline"; warns against overpromising and info-marketer dishonesty; conviction in high-ticket selling.
- Story: left job → gym → broke (slept on gym floor) → Gym Launch (sold the know-how) → refunds ~25% / partner fraud / processor freeze / scattered focus → focus on one business → sold ~66% of Gym Launch + Prestige Labs 2021 at ~$46.2M valuation → Acquisition.com with Leila Hormozi.
