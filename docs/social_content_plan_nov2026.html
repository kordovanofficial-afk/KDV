Hooks for the first 2 seconds of every Reel

  • Proof: "This wallet is 2 years old. This one arrived yesterday." · "Cut it open and you'll see the difference."
  • Problem: "If your wallet looks like this, watch this." (bulky wallet) · "Your gloves crack every winter? Here's why."
  • Curiosity: "What 6 cards, cash and your CNIC look like in a slim wallet." · "Packing today's orders: guess the bestseller."
  • Trust: "What you order vs what actually arrives at your door."

Voice: calm, confident, short sentences. English for the brand line, Roman Urdu for everyday talk (we test both and keep what works). Never shout, never invent reviews, never mention a city, and the Diva Tote is vegan.

5 · How organic and ads work together

  • Everyone who engages with a post becomes part of the MIDDLE-tier ad audience (people who know us), so organic makes the ads cheaper.
  • Every month, the organic post with the best saves and shares becomes next week's ad TEST.
  • The link in bio and story links carry tracking tags (utm_source=instagram), so we can see real orders from organic posts.
