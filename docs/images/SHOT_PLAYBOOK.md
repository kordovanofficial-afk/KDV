# Kordovan Shot Playbook — per-product Shots 2 & 5

Shots **1 (front), 3 (open), 4 (macro)** are the CATALOG UNIFORM — identical
Alabaster background, Kelvin and light direction on every product (§07 of
`docs/seo/SEO_ARCHITECTURE.html`). Never personalized.

Shots **2 (scale/wearing)** and **5 (lifestyle habitat)** are CONTEXT shots —
generated per product by `gen_shotsheet.py` from the rules below and embedded
in `shot-sheet.html`'s copy buttons.

## The art-direction lock (every 2 & 5 prompt ends with this)
Warm directional window light · golden, quiet tones · Quiet Heritage palette
(alabaster/parchment neutrals, walnut, brass, linen, stone) · shallow depth of
field · photoreal editorial · product kept 100% identical to the upload.
**Scenes vary. Atmosphere never does.**

## Shot 5 — the product's natural habitat (rotates within category so grid neighbours never twin)
- **Wallets:** café marble table beside an espresso → walnut desk with linen notebook & brass pen → evening dresser tray with watch & keys
- **Cowboy hats:** wooden fence rail at golden hour → worn saddle in warm barn light → held against the thigh on a dusty path
- **Jackets:** draped over a vintage leather armchair in a study → coat rack beside leather boots → worn at dusk on a quiet street, city blurred
- **Bags (work):** office desk beside a closed laptop and morning papers → café bench beside a coffee, commute pause
- **Bags (travel):** open mid-pack on a linen-dressed bed → car boot with a folded coat, weekend departure
- **Handbags/clutches:** marble console with perfume & pearls → restaurant table beside a candle
- **Shoes:** beside a leather armchair with a shoe horn → stone doorstep in autumn light
- **Belts:** coiled on a walnut dresser beside a watch → wardrobe rail with pressed shirts
- **Caps:** coat hook with a wool scarf → café table beside reading glasses
- **Gloves:** car console beside keys → flat-lay with overcoat and scarf
