The Kordovan Funnel

How every rupee of Meta spend moves a stranger to a delivered order: the tiers, what each one is for, which ads, formats, hooks and styles go where, how we judge them, and when we stop, keep or scale. Blessed Friday runs inside this system.

Live from 13 Nov 2026 at PKR 3,000/day · PKR 5,000/day during Blessed Friday (26–30 Nov) and from 1 Dec

1 · The funnel at a glance

One rule ties it together: every tier is judged on real Shopify orders (each ad has its own tracking tag) and, 14 days later, on whether those orders were delivered, not refused. Meta's own count is never used alone: it over-reports by about 2x.

Why a funnel, and what it fixes for us

  • Cheaper new customers: cold ads only have to start interest; warm tiers finish the job at a fraction of the cost.
  • Fewer COD refusals: the middle tier answers "is it real leather / will what arrives match?" before the order, not at the door. October lost 25% of orders.
  • We learn what works where: each ad is tagged by format, hook and style, so after 4–6 weeks we know, with evidence, which combination sells in which tier.
  • No more guessing on pauses: stop/keep/scale rules are written down in advance (section 5).

4 · Every ad is tagged by what's in it

The ad's name is also its tracking tag in Shopify: tier_product_format_hook_style_version, e.g. mof_mocha_orderedvsarrived_trust_real_v1.

DimensionValues we use
Formatcomparison · carousel · static · video · receipt · objection · orderedvsarrived · faq · closeup · ugc · countdown · ourmistake
Hookvalue · saving · proof · trust · status · gift · problem · deadline · craft
Stylelifestyle (dark heritage) · studio (clean product) · real (phone/UGC) · textled (headline-first)
Placement (from Meta)Feed · Stories · Reels: read per ad every week (we run 4:5 only)

This lets us answer: Which hook wins on cold audiences for wallets? Does real phone footage beat lifestyle on warm audiences? Do 4:5 ads sell in Stories? A pattern only counts once it has won in two or more ads.

5 · When to stop, when NOT to stop, when to scale

Break-even is per product: profit per order placed, after cost, delivery and that product's refusal rate. It is refreshed with Aug–Oct data before the first weekly read (e.g. Mocha Mate ≈ 990, gloves 1,000, Aristocrat ≈ 3,000).

SituationDecision
New ad, spent less than its break-even, no sale yetKeep. Too early.
Cold CTR below 0.6% after 3,000 impressionsStop: the hook failed.
Clicks and add-to-carts, but no sale yetKeep up to 2× break-even.
1.5× break-even spent, 0 Shopify ordersStop.
7-day cost per order above 1.3× break-evenStop, or move a trust ad to a warmer tier.
A winner has 1–2 bad daysKeep. Judge on 7 days, never on 1.
7-day cost per order ≤ 0.7× break-even with 3+ ordersScale +20%, then wait 72h.
Seen too often (frequency >3 cold / >6 warm) and CTR down 30%Refresh: new creative, same winning hook.
Refusal rate 10+ points above average (day 14)Stop even if it looks cheap.

Always: one change per ad set per 72h, batched; never judge an ad set without listing every ad in it; every pause or scale goes to the Founder as a per-ad table for a yes.
