Steal map

What we take from each global leader.


BrandThe lesson we steal
BellroyBenefit-first copy + warranty badge above the fold; show capacity/function, not specs.
SaddlebackHeirloom story + lifetime warranty as a hero pillar. "Built to outlive you."
Carl FriedrikEditorial whitespace + press/trust logo strip. Premium = restraint.
CuyanaWarm cream canvas + oversized imagery + a clear ethos line.
Nappa DoriHeritage-as-premium — we're the Pakistani answer, not a discount brand.
ShinolaName the silhouettes + "Made in Pakistan" as a badge.
Tanner GoodsMaker/workshop imagery — show the hands, not just the product.
Popov LeatherAn "Our Leather" education page + monogramming upsell.
WP StandardFeel-the-grain photography; colour-match the site to the product.
HidesignKeep the catalogue navigable and category-led as range grows.
