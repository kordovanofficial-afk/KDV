Genuine Leather Wallets, Made in Pakistan

Every Kordovan wallet is cut and hand-stitched from full-grain and Crazy Horse cow leather — the highest grade of hide available. Unlike bonded or corrected leather, full-grain keeps the natural surface of the skin intact, so it resists wear, breathes, and develops a rich personal patina the longer you carry it.

From slim cardholders and minimalist vertical bifolds to RFID-blocking smart wallets, the collection is built around one idea: an everyday carry that's good enough to be handed down. Saddle-stitched seams, burnished edges, and reinforced stress points are standard on every piece — not premium add-ons.

How to choose your wallet

If you carry only a few cards and value slimness, reach for a cardholder or the Luke vertical bifold. If you want maximum capacity with classic styling, a full bifold like the Bronco or Saffiano is ideal. Want contactless security? The KODO smart wallet blocks RFID skimming while staying pocket-slim. Every wallet ships free over PKR 2,500, with cash on delivery nationwide and a lifetime craftsmanship warranty.
