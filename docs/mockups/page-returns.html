Simple, fair, no fine print.

7-Day Returns

Easy Exchanges

Want a different colour or model? We're happy to exchange, subject to availability — any price difference is settled at the time.

Damaged? On Us.

The Details

What's eligible.

Eligible for return

  • Unused items in original condition, within 7 days of delivery
  • All original tags and packaging intact
  • Damaged or incorrect items reported within 48 hours
  • Manufacturing defects, any time, under our craftsmanship promise

Not eligible

  • Items used, worn, or showing signs of wear
  • Returns requested more than 7 days after delivery
  • Items missing tags or original packaging
  • Personalised or monogrammed pieces (unless faulty)
