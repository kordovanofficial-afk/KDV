Good to Know

Shipping questions, answered.

When will my order be dispatched?

Orders are typically packed and dispatched within 1–2 business days. You'll get a tracking number as soon as it leaves us.

Do you ship internationally?

Our standard delivery covers all of Pakistan. For international orders, message us on WhatsApp and we'll arrange a quote.

What if I'm not home for delivery?

The courier will attempt delivery and call you to reschedule. You can also coordinate a convenient time directly with them using your tracking details.

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

If your order hasn't shipped yet, contact us on WhatsApp with your order number and we'll update it right away.
