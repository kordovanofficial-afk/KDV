Leather Care

Care for it, and it lasts a lifetime.

Genuine leather is a living material — it breathes, flexes, and develops a beautiful patina over time. A little care keeps your Kordovan piece looking better with every year.

The Essentials

Your care routine, at a glance.

Every Day

  • Wipe with a soft, dry cloth after use to remove dust and moisture
  • Let wet leather dry naturally — never a hair dryer or heater
  • Keep out of prolonged direct sunlight
  • Avoid sharp objects and rough surfaces

Every 4–6 Weeks

  • Apply a small amount of quality leather conditioner with a soft cloth
  • Work in small circular motions, then buff off any excess
  • Conditioning keeps leather supple and prevents cracking

When Storing

  • Store in a cool, dry place away from humidity
  • Use a dust bag or breathable cotton cover — never plastic
  • Stuff bags with tissue paper to hold their shape
Embrace the Patina

The marks make it yours.

Our veg-tan and full-grain leathers are meant to darken and develop character over time. The scratches, the colour deepening, the softening — these aren't flaws.

They're the leather recording your life. This is what makes each piece uniquely yours, and no conditioner can buy it — only time and use.

Golden Rules

Do this, never that.

Do

  • Condition every 4–6 weeks to replenish natural oils
  • Let wet leather air-dry at room temperature
  • Store in a breathable dust bag in a cool, dry place
  • Treat scuffs gently — most fade into the patina

Don't

  • Use chemical cleaners, alcohol, or acetone — they strip the finish
  • Soak or submerge leather in water
  • Store in plastic or humid spaces — leather must breathe
  • Fold or crease bags for long periods
Know Your Leather

Care by material.

Full-Grain

The most durable, breathable hide — it keeps the natural grain, resists moisture, and ages most gracefully. Condition lightly; let it patina.

Vegetable-Tanned

Tanned with natural plant extracts. It darkens dramatically with sun and use — lean into it, and condition to keep it supple.

Crazy Horse

A waxed full-grain finish that shows light scratches by design. Rub a mark with your finger and it blends back in — that's the character.
