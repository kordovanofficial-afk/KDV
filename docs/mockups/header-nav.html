Why category-first (not Men/Women)
· Shoppers search by product type ("a wallet", "a jacket") — matches the best leather brands (Bellroy, Cuyana, Carl Friedrik).
· Your catalogue is accessories-led and skews men's — a Men/Women split would leave a thin, empty-looking Women's section.
· Gender still lives inside each mega menu as columns (Men's / Women's) — the hybrid that converts best.
