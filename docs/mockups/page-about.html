Our Story

Built to be handed down.

Kordovan was born from a simple belief: Pakistan deserves world-class leather — crafted right here at home, by Pakistani hands, for the people who carry it.

Where it began

It started with a belief.

Founded in Karachi, we set out to prove that genuine leather — the kind that gets better with age, that develops a rich patina, that outlasts trends — did not have to come from Europe or cost a fortune.

It could be made by Pakistani hands, for Pakistani people, and appreciated the world over. One wallet at a time, that belief became Kordovan.

Our Craft

From hide to stitch, by hand.

Every Kordovan piece starts with the finest cut of the hide and passes through trained hands at every step — cutting, skiving, saddle-stitching, burnishing, and finishing.

Where mass producers rely on machines and shortcuts, we rely on people and an unwillingness to compromise.
