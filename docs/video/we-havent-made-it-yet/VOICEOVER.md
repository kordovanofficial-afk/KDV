# Voiceover

**Record a real person. Do not use TTS.** Pakistani-English synthetic voices are poor,
and it would be the one fake thing in a film whose whole argument is authenticity.
A man in his fifties from the workshop, a phone six inches away, quiet room, three
takes per line.

## Voice brief

> A Karachi man in his fifties. Urdu is his first language; his English is fluent and
> everyday — confident, not careful.
>
> **He is not solemn.** He is a little proud and slightly amused — he half-smiles at
> "we have not made it yet", because he knows it is a strange thing to say. Read it as
> if explaining to a customer standing in front of you, not to a camera.
>
> **Pakistani, not Indian.** Flatter melody and heavier stress: he lands hard on one
> word and lets the rest of the sentence drop away, rather than rising and falling
> across the phrase. Keeps /v/ and /w/ distinct. Urdu arrives where it is simply the
> better word, not as decoration. Conversational, not composed — he starts again
> mid-thought, repeats for emphasis, says "no, no". **Keep those. Keep the breaths.**
>
> **Keep the take where he sounds least like he is reading.**

## Script — 4 lines, ~50 words, 27 seconds

| Scene | In–out | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:04 | "This? This is your jacket." *(beat)* "No, no. We have not made it yet." |
| 2 | 0:04–0:08 | "One hide. Cut to your measurement — not small, medium, large." |
| 3 | 0:08–0:12 | *silence — room tone only* |
| 4 | 0:12–0:17 | "Seven days. One man — start to finish. Haath ka kaam." |
| 5 | 0:17–0:20 | *silence — leather and zip only* |
| 6 | 0:20–0:23 | "Bas. Now it is yours." |
| 7 | 0:23–0:27 | "Made in Karachi. Half to begin, half at your door." |

**The commercial facts live in the captions, not the voice.** He never reads a price.
That is what keeps him sounding like a man talking rather than an advertisement.

**Scenes 3 and 5 are silent on purpose.** Four lines across 27 seconds, not seven.
If he talks over everything it becomes a lecture; the silences are what make the
lines land.

## Every scene, without exception

**NO MUSIC OF ANY KIND.** Voiceover and natural sound only — room tone, the scrape of
chalk, the machine, leather under a thumb.
