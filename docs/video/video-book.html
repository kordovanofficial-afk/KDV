Leather doesn't wear out. It wears in.

How every Kordovan video looks, sounds and gets made: ads, Reels, product films and founder content. Shoot it, edit it and check it against this book before it goes live.

Rule zero
Editing cannot rescue footage. The shoot is the video.
  1. Never edit a video from footage that wasn't shot for it. Reshoot (20 min) instead.
  2. AI voice and generated music are for drafts only. Final cuts use real voices, supplied tracks and recorded foley.
  3. We don't imitate fashion houses. Our style comes from honest leather in real hands.

02 · Non-negotiable

The five pillars

If a video has none of these, it isn't a Kordovan video.

Real handsLeather is a touch product. Most shots hold, open, flex or stitch something.
Side lightOne soft source from the side so the grain throws tiny shadows. Never overhead office light.
TextureAt least one macro per video: grain, burnished edge, stitch line, brass.
Honest demoThe product does its job on camera. It fills, snaps, closes, fits, layers.
TimePatina and years of wear. The one thing plastic and metal can't show.
03 · The visual grammar

Frame, shots, light and surface

Master everything in 9:16 at 1080×1920, then reframe 4:5 and 1:1 cuts from it. Keep text and product inside the band the Reels interface never covers.

  • Product in the middle third. Never low in frame, where the CTA bar covers it.
  • Camera locked off by default (a tripod or a stack of books). Stillness reads as quality.
  • Allowed moves: a slow push-in or pull-back, a slow rotation by hand. Handheld only for founder and on-body shots.
  • Never: whip pans, drones, "cinematic" orbits round a wallet.

Shot vocabulary use these codes in every shot list

MAC
MacroGrain, stitch, edge, snap, zip pull. Phone 2–5 cm away. Proves quality.
HAND
In handWaist height, plain surface behind. Scale and the default demo.
FILL

MECH

PAT
PatinaNew beside aged, a slow tilt in window light. Our answer to Ridge.
WEAR

MAKE
WorkshopCutting, skiving, stitching, burnishing, stamping. Craft and made-to-order.

To cameraFounder or team, chest-up. Trust.
FLAT
Flat layOn a styled surface. Catalogue stills and end frames.
Light recipe 1 · default

Window

Product 1 m from a window, light from the side. Hang a sheet over it if the sun is direct. A white card opposite fills the shadows. Switch off every ceiling light: mixed colour makes leather grey.

Light recipe 2 · night / mood

One lamp

A warm desk lamp from the side and slightly behind, the room otherwise dark, black card opposite. This is how to get the "luxury" look, in camera rather than in the edit.

Light recipe 3 · jackets

Golden hour

The hour before sunset, the subject turned three-quarters to the sun. Never midday.

Surfaces we use

  • Walnut or dark wood, raw linen
  • Slate, stone, concrete, kraft paper
  • A leather offcut, the workshop bench
  • One prop that belongs: keys, a CNIC sample, notes, a logo-free watch

Never in frame

  • Laptops, keyboards, monitors
  • White office desks, clutter
  • Branded mugs, phones or watches
  • Mixed lighting, ceiling tubes
Hands and wardrobe

Clean, trimmed nails. Neutral long sleeves (navy, olive, charcoal, cream). No logos. For jackets: dark denim or chinos, a plain tee or knit, clean boots. The jacket is the only statement.

Colour and grade

Grade lightly: true leather colour, slightly warm, shadows that keep detail, cognac that stays cognac. If footage needs heavy grading to look good, it was lit wrong. Reshoot. No crushed blacks, teal-orange, oval vignettes or fake grain.

05 · What it sounds like

Sound

Most Reels are watched with sound on, but every video must still work muted: captions plus a hook you can see.

Our signature

Foley, recorded on set

Record on a phone voice memo, 5–10 cm away, room silent: the brass snap, leather creak, a card sliding into a slot, a zip, thread pulled tight, an edge being burnished. Mix it louder than you think.

Voice

Real humans only

The founder or the team, in the Urdu/English mix people actually speak. AI voices are for drafts and timing only. Never final.

Music

Supplied, under the voice

Copyright-free tracks the owner supplies, or Meta's Sound Collection at upload. Sit it about −18 to −22 dB under the voice. Generated placeholder music is never final.

Silence

Let it land

One quiet second before a snap makes the snap land. Silence is allowed.

6–10 s · hooks, Stories

F6

Jacket fit and movement

15–30 s · jacket TOF + RT-J

Real bodies, the shoulder seam, layering, movement, close details, and the made-to-order reason. See chapter 08.

F7

Customer voice

15–30 s · retarget

Real buyers only, with permission, on their own phone. A screen-recorded WhatsApp voice note over product shots works. Never script, compose or reward a review.

F8

Myth-buster: real vs fake

20–30 s · TOF

Only tests that are true and safe: water beads, bend and crease recovery, cut-edge fibres vs PU mesh. No flame tests, no damage, no competitor names.

F9

Catalogue stills set

feeds catalogue ads, our cheapest clicks

Five stills per product, on the same surface and in the same light across the whole range: FLAT · HAND · open/inside · MAC · WEAR. In August 2026 catalogue ads cost roughly a quarter as much per click as static or video ads. Good stills aren't optional.

08 · Rs 22,000–35,000 · made to order

Jackets: remove the risk of not trying it on

Jackets are paid online and reach the door in about 3 weeks. A jacket video exists to answer "will it fit me, and is it worth the wait?"

Must show
  • Fit on two builds (slim and broad): front 3/4, side, back.
  • The shoulder seam sitting on the shoulder edge. This is the fit tell.
  • Layering: over a tee, then over a knit (September vs January).
  • Movement: walk, sit, arms up, ride.
  • Details: zip and pull, the lining (call it the lining), collar, cuffs, hardware.
Must say
  • Terms with the reason: "Each jacket is cut for one person, so it's paid online and ready in about 3 weeks."
  • Sizing: "Not sure of your size? WhatsApp us before you pay." This is the exact objection that cost October's jacket sales.
  • Never: a COD claim, "4–7 days", or "quilted" unless the outside really is.
  • Never name our base city. Show locations, never caption them as where we're from.

Setting: golden hour outdoors (rooftops, old streets, highway lay-bys, northern hills) or the workshop cutting table. Wear framing: layering and wind in the north, style in the mild south.

09 · Free kit, phone only

The shoot

Any recent phone, a tripod (or books), white card, black card, a warm desk lamp, a walnut or linen board, a lens cloth, and a second phone for foley.

Phone settings
  • 4K 30 fps normally; 4K 60 fps (or 1080p 120) for anything you'll slow down.
  • Lock exposure and focus, then pull exposure down 0.3–0.7.
  • Shoot vertical, gridlines on, HDR off, no digital zoom (move closer).
Discipline
  • Hold every shot 10 s before and after the action.
  • Shoot every shot 3 times.
  • Clean the lens every setup; wipe prints off leather and hardware.
  • Condition the leather the day before.
  • End with a foley pass: every sound, 3 takes each.

10 · Cutting it

Edit rules

PartShot lengthRule
Hook0.5–1 s cutsFast, then calm by 0:05.
Demo1.5–3 sReal time by default; time-lapse only for FILL.
Craft (F4)1.5–2.5 sEach shot paired with its own foley.
Patina / macro3–4 sLet it breathe.
End card2–3 sProduct, name, price, one trust line, CTA. Small horse mark in Brass or Espresso. The logo never opens a video.
Transitions

Hard cuts and match cuts (snap to snap, hand to hand). Dip to black only into the end card. No wipes, spins, zoom-blur or glitch.

Deliverables

9:16 master; 4:5 and 1:1 reframed (not letterboxed); a captioned and a no-text version.

Export

H.264 high, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 12–20 Mbps, AAC 48 kHz, about −14 LUFS. Strip GPS and metadata.

File names: KV_[Format]_[Product]_[Hook]_[Length]_[Ratio]_v#.mp4, for example KV_F2_MochaMate_H1_15s_9x16_v1.mp4.

11 · How we judge a video

Testing

Each shoot goes into one ad set as 3 hooks at the best length, so the hook is the only variable. Leave it alone for 72 h. Judge on 14-day windows; real ROAS is about half of what Meta reports. Kill rules and break-even CPAs live in the ads playbook.

Hook rate≥ 25%3-second views ÷ impressions
Hold rate≥ 25%15 s ThruPlays ÷ 3-second views
Link CTR≥ 1.5%video · ≥ 3% catalogue
Click → add to cart≥ 3%the account's real ceiling

Working targets. Re-baseline after the first ten videos.

Low hook rateWrite a new hook for the same body.
Good hook, weak holdFix the body: shorter shots, a clearer demo.
Good CTR, weak add-to-cartIt's the product page, not the video.

Refresh when frequency passes about 4, or when the hook rate drops 30% from week one. Swap the hook, not the whole video.

12 · Non-negotiable

Claims and compliance

Only claims verified for that exact product in its live Shopify data.

!Never mention Karachi. Say "our workshop" or "made in Pakistan".
!COD only on ready-made pieces. Jackets and footwear are paid online, always with the reason.
!Free delivery only "over Rs 5,500". Never unqualified.
!Lead time: "about 3 weeks to your door" for jackets and footwear. Nothing else.
!"Lifetime craftsmanship warranty". Never on The Stallion boot (its own terms say 1 year).
!No fabricated reviews, testimonials or counts. Sales numbers re-checked in Shopify that week.
!Croc and snake belts are textured cowhide. Never imply exotic skin.
!Vegan products (Diva Tote, Vibe LITE) are never called leather.
!No competitor names and no "better than X". Show it instead.
!Craft claims per product. Not everything is hand-stitched; check before you say it.

15 · Ours at any size

The eight constants

You should be able to tell it's Kordovan with the sound off and the logo covered, whether it's a Rs 1,499 keychain or a Rs 35,000 jacket. The kit in tools/video-edit/kv-kit/ applies 3–8 automatically.

1 · Frame oneProduct or problem, side-lit, in the safe band. It's the thumbnail.
2 · LightOne warm side source, true leather colour.
3 · TypeFraunces headline, 5 words max. Inter labels and captions.
4 · One accentA single italic word per video, in tan on dark or cognac on light.
5 · CaptionsPhrase by phrase, the spoken word brightens. Never box highlights.
6 · One curveEase-out entrances (0.6 s), ease-in-out camera. No bounce, no shake.
7 · Sonic logoOur real brass snap lands on the horse mark every time.
8 · End cardEspresso, product, name, price, one trust line, CTA. 2–3 s.

Zoom and camera moves call them by code in briefs

Approved transitions

  • Hard cut: about 80% of cuts
  • Match cut: snap → snap, new → aged wallet (1–2 per video)
  • Velocity cut: a hand leaves one shot and continues into the next
  • Warm fade-through: a time jump or into the end card (max 1)
  • Light leak: warm amber, Tier L only (max 1)
  • Editorial flash: launches only (max 1)
  • Iris: the secret-pocket reveal, F5 only

Never

  • Glitch, RGB split, pixelate, VHS
  • Vortex, ripple, gooey morphs, 3D flips
  • Slide/push wipes, zoom blur, whip pans
  • Camera shake, orbits, drift on every shot
  • Typewriter, scramble, neon, emoji-pop or particle text
  • Digital zoom above 115% (4K) or 108% (1080p)
Kinetic type

Headlines build word by word and lock centre. The accent word gets a thin cognac underline sweep, never a filled box. Emphasis words switch to Fraunces italic.

Fonts

Fraunces and Inter only. Roman Urdu is set in Inter. Urdu script is the one exception: Noto Nastaliq Urdu (free, open licence). Grain only at 3% or less on solid espresso type cards, to stop banding after compression. Never on footage.

18 · Directional, then proven by our own hook and hold rates

What makes it spread, our way

Pattern interrupt · every 1.5–2.5 s

A cut, a punch-in on a foley hit, or a new 3-word line. Something changes; nothing jitters.

Open loop

"Same wallet. 6 months apart." Hold back the aged one until 0:06.

Loop ending

Micro-clips end on the frame they opened with, so they replay seamlessly.

Satisfying payoff

Snap, zip, cards dropping in, burnishing. Real foley, slowed with Z5.

Share trigger

Myth-busters: "how to tell real leather" is what people forward before Eid.

Comment trigger

An honest Roman Urdu question: "Kaunsa colour? Mocha ya black?"

Native first second

Looks like a person filming: hands, a real room, no logo, no title card.

Save trigger

15 s care tips (conditioning, rain). Saves tell the algorithm the video is worth keeping.

Series

"Workshop Diaries 03": viewers recognise the format before they see the brand.

  • Fake countdowns or "only 2 left"

  • Flame tests
