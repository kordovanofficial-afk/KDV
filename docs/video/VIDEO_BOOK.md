# Kordovan Video Book

**This is the core file for every Kordovan video:** ads, Reels, product films and founder content.
Read it before you plan, shoot, edit or brief anything that moves.

It was written Oct 26 2026 after a failed attempt at a "Prada-style" film. The reasons it failed run through every rule below.

- Companion files:
  - `docs/brand/BRAND_MOODBOARD.md` (palette, type, art direction)
  - `ops/ADS_PLAYBOOK_PK.md` (economics, kill rules)
  - `docs/video/MOCHA_MATE_FOUNDER_SCRIPT.md` (a worked example of the founder format)
- ⚠️ `ops/ADS_PLAYBOOK_PK.md` §6 is stale: free delivery is **Rs 5,500**, not 2,500, and Easypaisa is gone.

## 0. The one lesson that comes first

> **Editing cannot rescue footage. The shoot is the video.**

The Oct 25–26 experiments proved it.

- **The stop-motion Mocha Mate ad** worked tolerably. It was honest, demo-led and fun.
- **The "Prada" film** failed.
  - The footage had been shot handheld at a desk, with a laptop and clutter behind the wallet, for a different purpose.
  - Heavy crops, a crushed-shadow grade and vignettes tried to hide that and made it look murky.
  - A synthetic voice and synthetic music made it feel fake.
  - **Luxury is a property of the light, the surface and the stillness in front of the lens.** It cannot be added in post.

Rules that follow from this:

1. **No video is edited from footage that was not shot for it.**
   If the footage isn't right, the next step is a 20-minute reshoot (§9), not a clever edit.
2. **Synthetic music and AI voice are drafts only.** Final cuts use:
   - a real voice (founder or team, Urdu/English);
   - copyright-free music the owner supplies;
   - real foley recorded on set.
3. **We do not imitate fashion houses.** Prada and Gucci sell a mood with a celebrity budget.
   We sell **an honest object that gets better with use**. Our style is earned from that, not borrowed.

## 2. The five pillars (if a video has none of these, it is not ours)

1. **Real hands.** Leather is a touch product. Most shots have a hand in them: holding, opening, flexing, stitching.
2. **Warm, directional light.** One soft source from the side, so the grain throws tiny shadows. Never flat overhead office light.
3. **Texture you can feel.** At least one macro per video: grain, edge burnish, stitch line, brass.
4. **An honest demo.** The product does its job on camera: fills, snaps, closes, fits, layers.
5. **Time.** Patina, wear, years. This is the thing plastic and metal cannot show.

## 3. Look: the visual grammar

### 3.1 Frame and safe zones (Meta / Instagram Reels, 1080×1920)
- **Master every video in 9:16 (1080×1920).** Cut 4:5 (1080×1350) and 1:1 versions from it for Feed.
- **Safe zone.** The app interface covers the top ~14% (profile, progress bar), the **bottom ~35%** (caption, CTA bar) and ~6% each side.
  - All text, faces and the product must sit inside the central band, roughly **y = 270 → 1250 px**.
  - Shoot with the product centred and leave headroom so the crop works.
- **The product sits in the middle third of the frame,** never at the bottom where the CTA bar will cover it.

### 3.2 Shot vocabulary (use these names in every shot list)
| Code | Shot | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **MAC** | Macro: grain, stitch, edge, snap, zip pull. Phone 2–5 cm from the leather, or the macro mode | Texture, quality proof |
| **HAND** | Product in hand, waist height, against a plain surface | Scale and default demo |
| **FILL** | Top-down: cards and cash going in, time-lapse or real time | Capacity (Bellroy's proof) |
| **MECH** | Close, locked off: one mechanism (snap, pocket, pull tab, zip) | Satisfying micro-clip, hooks |
| **PAT** | Side by side: new vs aged, slow tilt in window light | Patina (anti-Ridge) |
| **WEAR** | On body: back pocket, jacket on a person walking, sitting, turning | Real life, fit |
| **MAKE** | Workshop: cutting, skiving, stitching, burnishing, branding stamp | Craft, origin, made-to-order reason |
| **FACE** | Founder or team to camera, chest-up | Trust, founder format |
| **FLAT** | Flat lay on a styled surface | Catalogue stills, end frames |

### 3.3 Camera
- **Locked off by default** (tripod, or the phone propped on books). Stillness reads as quality.
- Allowed moves:
  - a slow push-in or pull-back by hand on a slider or table;
  - a slow product rotation by hand;
  - handheld only in FACE and WEAR, where it reads as real.
- **No whip pans, no drone, no "cinematic" orbits** around a wallet.

### 3.4 Light (the free recipes)
- **Window recipe (default):** the product 1 m from a window, light from the side. Hang a white sheet or curtain over the window if the sun is direct.
  A white card or paper on the other side fills the shadows. Turn off every ceiling light (mixed colour temperature makes leather look grey).
- **Lamp recipe (night or moody):** one desk lamp with a warm bulb, from the side and slightly behind. Room otherwise dark. Black cloth or card opposite to deepen shadows.
  This is the right way to get the "luxury" look, *in camera*.
- **Golden hour (jackets, outdoors):** the hour before sunset, the subject turned three-quarters to the sun. Never midday sun.

### 3.5 Surfaces and set (the biggest single fix)
- ✅ **Use:** walnut or dark wood, raw linen, slate or stone, a leather offcut, kraft paper, concrete, a workshop bench.
- 🚫 **Never:** a laptop, keyboard, monitor, white office desk, branded mugs or phones, clutter. If it is in frame it is in the ad.
- One prop at most, and it must belong:
  - for wallets: keys, a CNIC (blurred or a sample), notes, a watch without a logo;
  - for jackets: a scarf or a motorbike helmet.

### 3.6 Hands and wardrobe
- Clean, trimmed nails. A neutral long sleeve (navy, olive, charcoal, cream). No logos, no smartwatch faces.
- Jackets: dark jeans or chinos, a plain tee or knit, clean boots. The jacket is the only statement.

### 3.7 Colour and grade
- **Grade lightly. The target is "true leather colour, slightly warm".**
  Shadows keep detail, skin looks like skin, and cognac stays cognac rather than orange.
- If footage needs heavy grading to look good, it was lit wrong. Reshoot.
- ffmpeg reference grade (use it as a ceiling, not a starting point):
  `eq=contrast=1.05:saturation=1.0:gamma=1.0,colorbalance=rs=0.03:bs=-0.03`
- 🚫 No crushed blacks, no teal-orange, no oval vignettes, no fake film grain over a phone image (the one exception, on type cards only, is §15.5).

## 4. On-screen type
- **Headline:**
  - Fraunces 600–800, at least 72 px on 1080 wide;
  - colour: Alabaster `#F7F2EA` on footage with a soft dark scrim, or Espresso `#241C16` on light;
  - maximum **5 words** on screen at once.
- **Label / eyebrow:** Inter 600–700, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.1–0.3em, at least 32 px, in Antique Brass `#B0894F` or Stone.
- **Captions** (every spoken word is captioned): Inter 600, at least 44 px, sentence case, white with a 60% espresso box, inside the safe band.
- **Accent:** Cognac `#A0623A` for one word per video at most (e.g. "It *ages.*").
- **Timing:** on screen for at least 0.5 s + 0.3 s per word. The closing line holds at least 2 s.
- Text animates in simply: a fade, or a 6–10 px rise. 🚫 No bouncing, spinning, glitch or typewriter effects in brand videos.
  The stop-motion sticker style is allowed only in the F5 fun cut (§6).
- **Prices:** `Rs 3,200` (never "PKR 3200/-").

## 5. Sound

Most Reels are watched with sound on, but every video must still make sense on mute. That means captions, plus a hook that works visually.

1. **Foley is our signature.** Record it on set with a phone voice-memo app, 5–10 cm away, room silent:
   - the brass snap;
   - leather creak and flex;
   - a card sliding into a slot;
   - a zip;
   - thread pulled tight through a hole;
   - an edge being burnished.

   These sounds make leather feel real through the phone. Mix them louder than you think.
2. **Voice.** A real human voice, the founder or team, in natural Urdu/English as people actually speak.
   AI voices (Kokoro etc.) are **for drafts and timing only, never final.**
3. **Music.**
   - Use only tracks the owner supplies as copyright-free, or Meta's Sound Collection chosen at upload. Every source and its licence verdict: §17.
   - Music sits under the voice and foley (about −18 to −22 dB under the voice).
   - Cut on the beat, but never let music drive a product demo.
   - 🚫 Synthesised placeholder music is never final.
4. Silence is allowed. One second of quiet before a snap makes the snap land.

## 6. The format library

Every shoot produces **one master plus cut-downs**: 45s → 30s → 15s → 6s. Each length gets **3 hooks** (§7).
So one shoot gives about 9–12 ad variants. That is our version of Ridge's volume.

### F1 · "Everything fits": capacity demo (Bellroy lane) · 15–30s · TOF
1. **0–2s hook:** a messy pile (cards, notes, CNIC, keys) next to the closed wallet. Text: "All of this. One wallet?"
2. **2–12s:** FILL, top-down, real time or a 3× time-lapse. Each item in, with its foley.
3. **12–20s:** close it, MAC on the profile edge to show it stays slim, then WEAR (slides into a back pocket).
4. **End:** FLAT plus name, price, the line "Cash on delivery available" (non-made-to-order only), and the warranty.

### F2 · "It wears in": patina proof (anti-Ridge) · 15–30s · TOF + RT · **flagship format**
1. **Hook:** PAT. A new wallet and a 6-month or 2-year one side by side, a slow tilt in window light. Text: "Same wallet. 6 months apart."
2. **Body:** MAC on the aged grain, then a thumb along stitching that is still tight. Then FACE: "Asli leather ghis-ta nahi, rang pakadta hai."
3. **Close:** "It doesn't wear out. It wears in." Then the lifetime craftsmanship warranty and the product.
4. Needs **real aged pieces.** Ask the team and loyal customers to lend 1–3 year-old Kordovan items for a day. That collection is a brand asset.

### F3 · Founder / team honesty (UGC lane) · 30–50s · TOF
- FACE in a real setting (the workshop is ideal), handheld is fine, natural speech, one product.
- Structure as in `MOCHA_MATE_FOUNDER_SCRIPT.md`: hook, 3 real uses, PAT, warranty, price and CTA.
- Always film 3 hooks.

### F4 · The making: craft macro · 15–30s · TOF + brand
1. MAKE shots in order: hide, cut, skive, stitch, burnish, stamp, finished piece in hand.
2. Each shot is 1.5–2.5 s, locked off, with its foley. No voice, or one line at the end.
3. Text: three words maximum per shot ("Cut by hand.", "Stitched tight.", "Made to last.").
- ⚠️ **Claims discipline:** only state what is true of *that* product (`ops/ADS_PLAYBOOK_PK.md` §7).
  If a product is not hand-stitched, show the machine honestly or don't show stitching at all.

### F5 · Mechanism micro-clip · 6–10s · hooks, Stories, catalogue add-ons
- One MECH shot, perfect foley, looped feel: snap open and closed, the hidden pocket, a smart-wallet pull tab, a jacket zip.
- Text: one line ("The pocket for things you can't lose.").
- The **fun cut** variant (stop-motion stickers, upbeat track, as in the Oct 25 Mocha Mate cut) is allowed here only.

### F6 · Jacket fit and movement · 15–30s · jacket TOF + RT-J
See §8. Real bodies, the shoulder seam, layering, movement, close details, and the made-to-order reason.

### F7 · Customer voice · 15–30s · RT
- Real buyers only, with permission, on their own phone (screen-recorded WhatsApp voice note + product B-roll is fine).
- The provenance rules in `CLAUDE.md` apply in full. 🚫 Never script, compose or reward a review.

### F8 · Myth-buster: real vs fake leather · 20–30s · TOF
- Show tests that are true and safe: water bead, bend and crease recovery, cut-edge fibres vs PU mesh, smell (described).
- 🚫 No flame tests and no damaging stunts. Never name the competitor; "most wallets at this price" is enough.

### F9 · Catalogue stills set (feeds the catalogue ads, which are our cheapest clicks)
For every product, 5 stills (§3.2): FLAT, HAND, open/inside, MAC, WEAR. Same surface and light across the whole range.
Catalogue ads ran at roughly a quarter of the cost per click of static/video in Aug 2026. **Good stills are not optional.**

## 8. Jackets: the extra rules

Jackets are made to order, Rs 22,000–35,000, paid online, delivered in about 3 weeks.
The video's job is to **remove the risk of buying something you can't try on.**

**Must show:**
1. **Fit on real bodies.** At least 2 builds (slim and broad), 3/4 front, side and back.
2. **The shoulder seam** landing on the shoulder. This is the fit tell.
3. **Layering.** Over a tee, then over a knit (the "September vs January" sizing lesson).
4. **Movement.** Walk, sit, raise arms, ride (helmet optional). Leather that moves well sells.
5. **Close details:**
   - the zip and its pull;
   - the lining (quilted viscose: say "lining", never call the jacket quilted unless it is);
   - collar and cuffs;
   - hardware.
6. **The made-to-order reason, with the terms.** "Each jacket is cut for one person, so it's paid online and ready in about 3 weeks." **Never the terms without the reason.**
7. **The sizing CTA:** "Not sure of your size? WhatsApp us before you pay." (This answers the exact objection that killed October's jacket sales.)

**Look and setting:**
- Golden hour outdoors: rooftop, old city street, highway lay-by, northern hills.
- Indoors: the workshop with the cutting table.

**Hard rules:**
- 🚫 **Never mention or caption any city as our base** (see the hard rule in `CLAUDE.md`). Locations are shown, never named as origin.
- 🚫 No COD claim on any jacket or footwear video. No "4–7 days" ever (dead).
- Wear framing by region, without naming our base: north = layering and warmth against wind; mild south = style. A "sell the style / sell the layering" split is fine.

## 9. The shoot kit (free) and phone settings

- **Kit:**
  - any recent phone;
  - a tripod (or books plus a phone clamp);
  - a white card or paper (fill);
  - a black card or cloth (negative fill);
  - a warm desk lamp;
  - a surface board (walnut or linen);
  - lens cloth;
  - a second phone for foley (voice memos).
- **Settings:**
  - 4K 30 fps for normal shots, 4K 60 fps (or 120 fps 1080p) for anything you'll slow down;
  - lock exposure and focus (long-press on the subject), then pull exposure down about 0.3–0.7 so the leather doesn't blow out;
  - gridlines on;
  - **vertical**;
  - HDR off for consistency;
  - no digital zoom: move the phone closer instead.
- **Discipline:**
  - every shot held **10 seconds** before and after the action;
  - every shot done **3 times**;
  - clean the lens before every setup;
  - wipe fingerprints off the leather and hardware;
  - condition the leather the day before (an hour, not minutes).
- **Foley pass:** at the end of each shoot, record every sound from §5.1 separately, 3 takes each.

### 9.1 The 20-minute wallet shoot (one product)
1. FLAT (5 s);
2. HAND rotate;
3. MECH snap ×3;
4. open and inside;
5. FILL top-down;
6. MAC grain;
7. MAC edge and stitch;
8. PAT with an aged piece;
9. WEAR back pocket;
10. FACE line ×3 hooks;
11. foley pass.

### 9.2 The 45-minute jacket shoot (one jacket)
1. On a hanger (FLAT-equivalent);
2. put it on: zip up, collar flick;
3. front, 3/4 and back on two builds;
4. shoulder seam close;
5. walk toward camera;
6. sit, stand, arms up;
7. over a tee, then over a knit;
8. MAC on zip, lining, cuff and hardware;
9. MAKE (if in the workshop): panel cutting, stitching;
10. FACE hooks ×3;
11. foley: zip, leather creak, snap.

## 10. Edit rules

- **Rhythm by format:**
  - hooks cut every 0.5–1 s;
  - demo shots 1.5–3 s;
  - craft (F4) 1.5–2.5 s;
  - PAT and MAC can breathe 3–4 s;
  - the whole video should feel calm by 0:05.
- **Cuts:** hard cuts and match cuts (snap to snap, hand to hand). A dip to black only into the end card.
  🚫 No wipes, spins, zoom-blur or glitch transitions. The approved transitions and zooms are named in §15.1–15.2.
- **Speed:** real time by default. Slow motion only on footage shot at 60–120 fps. Time-lapse only for FILL.
- **End card (2–3 s):** product FLAT or HAND, name, price, one trust line (warranty, or COD where true), and the CTA.
  Horse mark small in Brass or Espresso. The logo never opens a video.
- **Deliverables per video:**
  - 9:16 master;
  - 4:5 and 1:1 cuts (reframed, not letterboxed);
  - a captioned version;
  - a no-text version (for Meta's own text tests).
- **Naming:** `KV_[Format]_[Product]_[Hook]_[Length]_[Ratio]_v#.mp4`, e.g. `KV_F2_MochaMate_H1_15s_9x16_v1.mp4`.
- **Export:** H.264, high profile, 1080×1920, 30 fps, about 12–20 Mbps, AAC 48 kHz, loudness about −14 LUFS. Strip GPS and metadata.

## 11. Testing and the numbers we judge by

- Each shoot goes into **one ad set as 3 hooks × the best length**, so the hook is the only variable.
- Leave it **72 h untouched** (August lost money to 7 learning resets in 11 days).
- **Creative health metrics** (working targets; re-baseline after the first 10 videos):
  | Metric | Definition | Target |
  |---|---|---|
  | Hook rate | 3-second views ÷ impressions | ≥ 25% |
  | Hold rate | ThruPlays (15 s) ÷ 3-second views | ≥ 25% |
  | Link CTR | link clicks ÷ impressions | ≥ 1.5% video, ≥ 3% catalogue |
  | Click → ATC | add to carts ÷ link clicks | ≥ 3% (the account's real ceiling) |
- **Low hook rate** means a new hook on the same body. **Good hook but low hold** means fix the body. **Good CTR but low ATC** means it's the product page, not the video.
- **Kill rules and break-even CPAs live in `ops/ADS_PLAYBOOK_PK.md` and `CLAUDE.md`.** Judge on 14-day windows. Real ROAS is about half of Meta-reported.
- **Refresh:** when frequency is above ~4 or the hook rate falls 30% from its first week, swap the hook, not the whole video.

## 12. Claims and compliance (non-negotiable)

- Only claims verified for **that product** in its Shopify data: material, stitching method, pockets, capacity, price.
- **Never mention Karachi.** Our base is "our workshop" / "made in Pakistan".
- **COD:** only on products that are not made to order. Jackets and footwear are paid online, and the reason is always stated with the terms.
- **Free delivery:** only "over Rs 5,500". Never unqualified.
- **Lead time** (jackets and footwear): "about 3 weeks to your door". Nothing else.
- **Warranty:** "lifetime craftsmanship warranty". Never on The Stallion boot (its own terms say 1 year).
- **No fabricated reviews, testimonials or counts.** "150+ sold this year" only if re-verified from Shopify that week.
- **Croc and snake belts are textured cowhide.** Never imply exotic skin.
- **Vegan products** (Diva Tote, Vibe LITE) are never called leather.
- **No competitor names, no "better than X".** Show, don't name.

## 15. The Kordovan signature: what makes a video ours at any size

A viewer should know it is Kordovan with the sound off and the logo covered.
These **eight constants never change**, whether it is a Rs 1,499 keychain or a Rs 35,000 jacket:

| # | Constant | The rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Frame 1** | Product or problem in the safe band, lit from the side. It is also the thumbnail. |
| 2 | **Light** | One warm side source, true leather colour (§3.4, §3.7). |
| 3 | **Type** | Fraunces headline, at most 5 words, plus Inter labels and captions. Nothing else. |
| 4 | **Accent** | Exactly one italic accent word per video (Tan `#C08B5C` on dark, Cognac `#A0623A` on light). |
| 5 | **Caption style** | Phrase-by-phrase weight-shift captions (§15.4). Never TikTok box highlights. |
| 6 | **Motion curve** | One ease family: entrances 0.6 s ease-out, camera moves ease-in-out. No bounce, no elastic, no shake. |
| 7 | **Sonic logo** | Our real brass snap lands on the horse mark in every end card (§17.1). |
| 8 | **End card** | Espresso ground, product, name, price, one trust line, CTA, small horse mark. 2–3 s. |

The kit that enforces 3–8 automatically is **`tools/video-edit/kv-kit/`**: `kv-brand.css` maps every installed HyperFrames block onto Quiet Heritage colours, our two fonts and our eases.

### 15.1 Zoom and camera-move vocabulary (named, so briefs can call them)
| Code | Move | Use | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Z1 Slow push** | 100 → 106% over the whole shot | default on MAC, PAT and stills (Ken Burns) | any shot over 2 s |
| **Z2 Punch-in** | cut-on-action jump 100 → 112% (or a 4-frame ease) | the moment of the claim: the snap, the price, the "6 months" | max 1 per 5 s, always on a sound hit |
| **Z3 Reveal pull** | 115 → 100% | show scale: wallet in hand → back pocket → whole outfit | once per video |
| **Z4 Dolly zoom** | `camera-dolly-zoom` block | jackets only, the hero on-body shot | once per film |
| **Z5 Speed ramp** | `beat-freeze-cut`: 100% → 40% on the payoff, 150–200% through repetition | snap closing, zip, card going in; speed through FILL | 60 fps+ footage only |
| **Z6 Hold** | nothing moves | founder line, patina tilt, end card | stillness is a move too |

🚫 **Banned moves:** zoom blur, whip pans, 3D spins, camera shake, rotating orbits, "drift" on every shot.
Digital zoom never goes above 115% on 4K footage (or 108% on 1080p), or the grain falls apart.

### 15.2 Transition library (installed in `kv-kit`)
| Transition | Block | When | Share of cuts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hard cut** | — | default | ~80% |
| **Match cut** | `match-cut`, `text-match-cut` | snap → snap, hand → hand, new wallet → aged wallet, one word becoming the next | the "wow" cut, 1–2 per video |
| **Velocity cut** | `cut-the-curve` | a hand moving out of frame continues into the next shot | hooks |
| **Warm fade-through** | `fade-through` (espresso wash) | a time jump (new → 6 months) or into the end card | max 1 |
| **Light leak** | `organic-light-leak-overlay` (warm amber only) | golden-hour jackets, patina "years passing" | max 1, Tier L only |
| **Editorial flash** | `editorial-flash-overlay` (warm, never white) | a launch reveal | max 1, launches only |
| **Iris reveal** | `iris-reveal` | the secret pocket or inside reveal only | F5 only |

🚫 **Never:** glitch, chromatic/RGB split, pixelate, vortex, ripple, VHS, 3D flip, slide/push wipes, gooey morphs.
These are installed by other brands to look busy. Ours earn attention with the leather, not the edit.

### 15.3 Kinetic type
- **Headline build:** `kinetic-center-build`. Words arrive one by one and lock centre. Use it for the 3–5-word headline only.
- **Key-word mark:** `inline-highlight` as a thin cognac underline sweeping under the accent word. Never a filled box.
- **Editorial emphasis:** `caption-editorial-emphasis`, with Inter for the line and the accent word switching to Fraunces italic.
- 🚫 No typewriter, scramble/decode, neon, gradient-fill, emoji-pop or particle-burst text.

### 15.4 Captions: our style
- **Phrase-by-phrase, 2–4 words**, with the spoken word brightening from 55% to 100% Alabaster (`caption-weight-shift`). It reads as premium, and still gives the word-by-word movement that agencies credit with lifting 3-second views (directional, unproven claims: test it, don't trust it).
- Inter 600 ≥ 44 px on a 62% espresso box, inside the safe band, centred, never above the product.
- **Roman Urdu** stays in Inter. **Urdu script** uses **Noto Nastaliq Urdu** (free, SIL Open Font License), the only third font allowed and only for Urdu script.

### 15.5 Grain and texture
- Never over footage (§3.7).
- Allowed **only** on solid espresso type cards and end cards, via `grain-overlay` at ≤ 3% opacity. It stops flat dark fields banding after Meta re-compresses them. Invisible on purpose.

## 16. Scale tiers: small product vs big product

Same eight constants, different tempo. Pick the tier from the price and the purchase weight.

| | **Tier S, small** | **Tier M, medium** | **Tier L, large** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Products** | wallets, cardholders, belts, keychains, caps (≤ Rs 6,500) | bags, laptop/office/travel (Rs 6,000–45,000) | jackets, footwear, launches, collections (made to order) |
| **Formats** | F1, F2, F5, F8, F9 | F1, F3, F4, F7 | F6, F4, F3, F2, F7 |
| **Length** | 6–15 s (master 30 s) | 15–30 s | 20–45 s (6 s teasers for retarget) |
| **Cut rhythm** | 1–1.5 s, punchier | 2–2.5 s | 2.5–4 s, let it breathe |
| **Hero shot** | MAC + MECH | HAND → WEAR (Z3 reveal pull for scale) | WEAR at golden hour, Z4 once |
| **Zooms allowed** | Z1, Z2, Z5 | Z1, Z2, Z3 | Z1, Z3, Z4, Z6 |
| **Transitions extra** | match cut, iris (F5) | match cut, fade-through | light leak, fade-through, editorial flash (launch) |
| **Sound** | foley-forward, music optional | foley + voice | voice + music bed + foley details |
| **Must say** | price; COD where true; free delivery over Rs 5,500 | capacity (what fits, e.g. 15.6-inch laptop) | made to order + the reason + about 3 weeks + WhatsApp sizing |
| **Pace of the viewer** | impulse: buy in one video | considered: two touches | considered: retarget sequence (F6 → F7 → F3) |

A **collection or brand film** is Tier L tempo with a Tier S shot inside it (one macro that snaps). That keeps a big film tactile.

## 18. What makes it spread (and our version of each)

Short-form research is mostly agency blogs, so treat the numbers as **directional, then prove them with our own hook and hold rates (§11).**

| Mechanic | Generic version | **Kordovan version** |
|---|---|---|
| **Pattern interrupt** every 1.5–2.5 s | random zooms, emojis | a cut, a Z2 punch-in on a foley hit, or a new 3-word line. Something changes, nothing jitters |
| **Open loop** | "wait till the end" | "Same wallet. 6 months apart." The aged one is withheld until 0:06 |
| **Loop ending** | — | F5 micro-clips end on the frame they started with, so they replay seamlessly (rewatches lift reach) |
| **Satisfying payoff** | slime, ASMR | the snap, the zip, cards dropping in, burnishing. Real foley, slowed via Z5 |
| **Share trigger** | shock | myth-busters (F8): "how to tell real leather" is the video people forward to a friend before Eid |
| **Comment trigger** | "comment YES" | an honest question in Roman Urdu: "Kaunsa colour? Mocha ya black?" |
| **Native first second** | — | looks like a person filming, not an ad: hands, real room, no logo, no title card |
| **Save trigger** | — | care tips (conditioning, rain) as 15 s F4 variants. Saves tell the algorithm it is worth keeping |
| **Series** | — | numbered episodes ("Workshop Diaries 03") so returning viewers recognise the format before the brand |

🚫 We don't use: fake countdowns, fake "only 2 left", stitched competitor bashing, flame tests, clickbait thumbnails that the video doesn't pay off.
