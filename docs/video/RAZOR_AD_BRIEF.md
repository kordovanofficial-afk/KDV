# Razor ad brief: "The Back-Pocket Brick" (Oct 27 2026)

Product: The Razor | RFID Smart Leather Wallet for Men. Rs 2,450 (compare-at 2,999), 6 colours,
67 in stock, 4.8 stars from 20 reviews. Cards sit inside a leather sleeve and come out with a thumb
push through the notch. Cash sits under the strap on the back. Not made to order, so COD is available.

## Why the earlier Razor ads failed
- They sold **RFID**. Very few Pakistani buyers carry contactless cards, so the fear doesn't land.
  Our own wallet blog article says RFID "does nothing" without a contactless card.
- They **explained** the wallet. Neither showed a problem the viewer feels in their own pocket.
- Results: TOF-B Razor spent 28,060 lifetime at 2.44x Meta (about 1.2x real). The second video
  dropped CTR from 1.77% to 0.98%.
- What has worked in this account: **founder voice** (TOF-M Mocha Mate founder videos producing real,
  UTM-tagged Shopify orders).
- ⚠️ The "Razor UGC post, 295 likes" was a paid UGC ad padded with bogus likes and fake comments
  (user, Oct 27 2026). It is NOT evidence that Roman Urdu wins. Language is therefore a test variable:
  run the winning hook in a Roman Urdu cut and an English-caption cut and let orders decide.

## The idea
Pakistani men carry a fat bifold in the back pocket and sit on it all day, on a bike or in a car.
The Razor goes in the front pocket. We don't say "minimalist" or "RFID". We show the brick, empty it,
and move what matters into the Razor in one take.

### Three hooks (same body, test all three)
- **H-A "Roz is pe baithte ho"**: founder sits on a car seat with the fat wallet in the back pocket and
  shifts uncomfortably. Text: "Aap roz is pe baithte ho."
- **H-B "The thud"**: the stuffed bifold drops on the table with a heavy thud, then the loaded Razor
  drops with a soft tap. Text: "Ek brick. Ek Razor."
- **H-C "Metal wallet"** (only if you can borrow a metal card wallet): tap it on the table, loud clank,
  then the Razor, silent. Text: "Metal nahi. Leather." Never name Ridge on screen or in copy.

## Shoot notes (you shoot, Claude edits)
- Face the window. Your test photos are backlit, which turns the leather muddy.
- Wooden table or the brown leather offcut. No bedsheet or tiles.
- Props: a really stuffed old bifold (borrow one), a ruler, jeans with a front pocket, a bike or car
  seat, all 6 Razor colours, real-looking cards.
- **Never show a real ID.** Your driving licence with its number was visible in the test photos.
  Use bank-style cards with the numbers turned away.
- Shoot each beat 3 times, phone locked at 1080p 30fps, and record VO in the same take or right after.
- Claude does the zooms, captions, music (CC0), SFX (thud, slide, snap), grade, end card.

## Money: the honest part
- Razor profit per PLACED order ≈ **Rs 1,029** (34% refusal, ADS_PLAYBOOK_PK.md).
- This account's cold CPA has been ≈ Rs 1,979, so an average ad loses money on the Razor.
  The ad must roughly halve CPA to break even.
- Test: one ad set, 3 ads (H-A/H-B/H-C), Rs 1,500/day, TOF-M targeting (9 cities, LLA 1%+3%,
  COD Refusers + Purchasers 180D + Delivered SEED excluded), Purchase optimisation, A+ creative off.
- Kill: Rs 2,500 spent with 0 Shopify orders by day 3. Pause any single hook with CTR under 1.5% at day 3.
- Scale: only if day-7 Shopify-verified placed CPA ≤ Rs 1,000 with ≥ 8 orders. Raise 20–30% every
  3 days to 5,000. At 5,000/day and CPA 1,000 that is ~35 orders/week, enough to leave learning.
- Never put a free-delivery badge on a Razor creative (2,450 is under the Rs 5,500 threshold).
