# Razor ad brief: three shoot styles (v2, Oct 27 2026)

Product: The Razor | RFID Smart Leather Wallet for Men. Rs 2,450 (compare-at 2,999), 6 colours,
67 in stock, 4.8 stars from 20 reviews. Cards sit inside a leather sleeve and come out with a thumb
push through the notch. Cash sits under the strap on the back. Not made to order, so COD is available.

## Why the earlier Razor ads failed
- They sold **RFID**. Very few Pakistani buyers carry contactless cards, so the fear doesn't land.
- They **explained** the wallet instead of showing a problem the viewer feels in their own pocket.
- Results: TOF-B Razor spent 28,060 lifetime at 2.44x Meta (about 1.2x real). The second video
  dropped CTR from 1.77% to 0.98%.
- What has worked in this account: **founder voice** (TOF-M Mocha Mate founder videos producing real,
  UTM-tagged Shopify orders). The old "Razor UGC post, 295 likes" had bogus likes and fake comments,
  so it proves nothing.

## The test: one message, three styles
Every video says the same thing: **the fat wallet lives in your back pocket and you sit on it all
day. The Razor goes in the front pocket.** Same product, same offer, same end card, same length
(20–25 s). Only the style changes, so the winner tells us which STYLE sells.

| | A. Founder to camera | B. Hands only, no talking | C. The doorbell skit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feels like | a person you trust telling you | a satisfying product demo | a short funny before/after story |
| Face | yes | no | yes, acting, no talking to camera |
| Voice | you talking, on camera | none, sound effects + music | two short lines, mostly sound |
| Where | table at home | wooden table, top-down | sofa, front door, hallway mirror |
| Gear | phone on a stack of books at eye level | phone directly above the table | phone on a shelf, one helper |

### Style A. Founder to camera (about 25 s)
One take, you holding both wallets. Talk the way you'd talk to a friend. Don't read a script; hit
these beats in your own words:

| Time | Picture | You say (roughly) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | You hold up a fat bifold | "Yeh aap ka wallet hai. Aur aap roz is pe baithte ho." |
| 3–10 | You empty it on the table | "Aadhi cheezein aap ne mahinon se nahi dekhi." |
| 10–17 | Cards in, cash under the strap, thumb pushes a card out | "Cards andar, cash peeche. Ek ungli se card bahar." |
| 17–22 | It goes into your front pocket | "Aur yeh aage ki pocket mein jata hai." |
| 22–25 | You hold it to camera | "Yeh hai Razor. Hum ne apni workshop mein banaya hai." |

Claude adds: captions, a zoom on the thumb push, the end card.

### Style B. Hands only, no talking (about 20 s)
Camera straight down on a wooden table. No face, no voice. The sounds carry it.

| Time | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Stuffed bifold drops on the table | heavy thud |
| 2–7 | Hands empty it: receipts, old cards, fall away | paper rustle |
| 7–13 | Cards slide into the Razor, cash goes under the strap | leather slide |
| 13–16 | Thumb pushes one card out, slow | soft click |
| 16–18 | Both wallets turned on their edge, ruler beside them | silence, then a tap |
| 18–20 | All 6 colours slide into frame | music lifts |

On-screen text (Claude): "Aap roz is pe baithte ho." → "Aadha kachra." → "Ek ungli." → (real mm) → "6 rang".

### Style C. The doorbell skit, all at home (about 25 s)
A two-part mini story with a before and an after, filmed in your own house. Everyone in Pakistan
knows this moment: the rider is at the door, cash on delivery, and you're digging through a fat wallet.
You need one helper to play the rider. Film from INSIDE the door, so only the rider's hand and the
parcel are seen (no face, no branded courier jacket).

| Time | Picture | Sound / line |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | You on the sofa. You shift, reach under you and pull out the fat wallet | "Uff." (that's the only word) |
| 3–5 | Doorbell. You get up | doorbell |
| 5–11 | BEFORE: at the door, rider's hand holds out the parcel. You dig through the fat wallet, receipts fall on the floor, the rider's hand taps the door frame | rider (off camera): "Bhai jaan, jaldi." |
| 11–12 | Hard cut, white flash. Caption: "Ab." | whoosh |
| 12–18 | AFTER: same door, same parcel. Razor comes out of your FRONT pocket, cash slides out from under the strap, paid in 2 seconds | leather slide, then "Shukriya." |
| 18–22 | Side view of you in the hallway mirror: front pocket flat, back pocket empty | quiet |
| 22–25 | End card | brass snap |

Claude adds: captions, the before/after flash, a "2 sec" timer on the after, the end card.
Shoot both halves in the same light and the same clothes, so the only thing that changes is the wallet.

### Same in all three
- End card: The Razor · Rs 2,450 · Cash on delivery · 6 colours, with the brass snap sound.
- Roman Urdu speech with English captions wherever anyone talks. Language gets its own test later, on the winner.
- Never name Ridge on screen or in copy.

## Shoot notes (you shoot, Claude edits)
- Face the window. Your test photos are backlit, which turns the leather muddy.
- **Never show a real ID.** Your driving licence with its number was visible in the test photos.
  Use bank-style cards with the numbers turned away.
- Props: a really stuffed old bifold (borrow one), a ruler, jeans with a front pocket, a parcel,
  a few banknotes, all 6 Razor colours, one helper for the rider's hand in Style C.
- Phone at 1080p 30fps, vertical. Shoot every beat 3 times.
- Claude does the zooms, captions, music (CC0), sound effects, grade, end card.

## Ad copy (same on all three)
Primary text:
> Back pocket mein mota wallet, aur roz us pe baithna. Razor mein cards andar, cash peeche strap mein,
> aur poora wallet aage ki pocket mein aa jata hai. Asli leather, chhe rang, Rs 2,450.
> Cash on delivery available.

- **Headline:** Aage ki pocket ka wallet
- **Description:** The Razor by Kordovan · Rs 2,450

## How we judge it (money)
- Razor profit per PLACED order ≈ **Rs 1,029** (34% refusal). This account's cold CPA has been
  ≈ Rs 1,979, so the winning style has to roughly halve that.
- **Three separate ad sets, Rs 1,000/day each** (not one ad set with 3 ads, or Meta picks a favourite
  on day one and the other two never get a fair spend). Same audience as the Mocha Mate ads:
  9 cities, lookalikes 1% + 3%, COD Refusers + Purchasers 180D + Delivered SEED excluded.
- **Kill a style:** Rs 2,500 spent with 0 Shopify orders by day 3.
- **Pick the winner at day 7:** lowest Shopify-verified cost per placed order, with at least 5 orders.
  Judge on orders, never on likes or views.
- **Then:** test openings and Roman Urdu vs English inside the winning style only, and scale it
  20–30% every 3 days toward Rs 5,000/day while cost per order stays at or under Rs 1,000.
- Never put a free-delivery badge on a Razor creative (2,450 is under the Rs 5,500 threshold).
