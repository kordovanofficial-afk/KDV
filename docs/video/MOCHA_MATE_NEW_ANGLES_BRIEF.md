## Ad A: "Asli hai ya nahi?" (three tests, about 22 s)

Buyers here worry most that the "leather" they ordered is rexine. Everyone knows the lighter test.
This ad answers that worry three times in the first 6 seconds. Each test is its own hook: if someone
scrolls past the fire, the water may stop them, and the key may stop them after that.

Camera straight down on a table. Hands only.

| Time | Beat | What we see | Sound and on-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Hook 1: Fire | Lighter flame passes under the wallet's edge, keeps moving. No melting, no bubbling | lighter click, flame · **"Lighter test."** |
| 2–4 s | Hook 2: Water | Water poured onto the wallet, wiped off once with a cloth | splash · **"Paani."** |
| 4–6 s | Hook 3: Key | Key drags across the leather, a thumb rubs the mark away | scratch · **"Chaabi."** |
| 6–8 s | Reveal | Wallet held up to camera, clean | **"Teeno test. Ek wallet."** |
| 8–16 s | Use | Opens, cards in, notes in, folds shut, side view to show it's slim | leather sounds · **"Asli leather. Roz ke liye."** |
| 16–19 s | Close | Into the pocket | quiet |
| 19–22 s | End card | Mocha Mate · Rs 3,200 · Cash on delivery | brass snap |

### Test before you film (off camera)

- **Fire:** try it on a leather offcut first, then very briefly on a spare Mocha Mate. Keep the flame moving. If it leaves a scorch mark, drop the shot.
- **Key:** a scratch that rubs out with a thumb only happens on oiled or pull-up leather. If the mark stays, swap this hook for **standing on the wallet with a shoe and lifting it, unharmed** (text: **"Jootay ke neeche."**).
- **Water:** wipe it straight away and check that it leaves no stain.

**Only film a test the wallet really passes.** A faked test brings refused parcels and angry comments.

### Three versions from one shoot

Claude cuts three versions from the same footage, each opening with a different test (fire first,
water first, key first). All three go in Ad A's ad set. Within a week we know which hook stops the
scroll, and we use it on the next products.

## Shoot checklist (both ads)

- Phone vertical, 1080p 30 fps. Light from a window, facing the wallet, never behind it.
- Shoot every beat **3 times**.
- **Never show a real ID.** Use a dummy card or turn the numbers away.
- Props: Mocha Mate (plus a spare for the fire test), lighter, glass of water, cloth, keys, a few cards and notes, gift box, a shoe (backup test).
- Same table, same light for the whole of each ad.
