0Fix the Shopify shipping rate

Before anything else · ~5 minutes

Shopify admin → Settings → Shipping and delivery → your delivery profile → Manage rates.

  1. Find the Rs 250 rate. Edit its condition: maximum order price from 2,499 → 5,499.
  2. Find the Free rate. Edit its condition: minimum order price from 2,500 → 5,500.
  3. Save both.
Change both, or checkout breaks If you only raise the free-shipping minimum, every basket between Rs 2,500 and Rs 5,499 falls into a gap with no matching rate and the customer sees "no shipping available". Both edits, same sitting.

1Facebook — comment auto-reply + auto-DM

The single highest-value switch in this document

This is the one that will change your day. When someone comments "price" under a Reel, Meta posts a public reply and opens a private message with that person — automatically, in seconds, at 3am.

Go to business.facebook.com → Inbox → Automations (left sidebar). Click Create automation.

If the menu looks different Meta moves this around. Look for anything named Automations, Automated responses, or Auto-reply to comments — inside Inbox, or under Business Suite → All tools → Inbox. It is the same feature under whichever name your account shows.

1a. The price automation

  1. Choose the template Comments on posts (or "Reply to comments" / "Comment to DM").
  2. Apply it to All posts and reels, not one specific post — otherwise you will be re-doing this every time you post.
  3. Set the trigger to specific keywords and paste the trigger list from the appendix.
  4. Turn ON both Reply in comments and Send a message.
  5. Paste the public reply and the DM from the appendix.
  6. Save and set it Live.
Write 3–4 variations of the public reply Meta rotates them. Identical repeated comments under the same post look robotic and Facebook can down-rank them. Four variations are supplied in the appendix — use all of them.

1b. Repeat for the other intents

Build one automation per intent, in this order of value:

  • Price — the flood (done above)
  • COD / delivery charges
  • Delivery time
  • "How do I order"
  • Order status / where is my parcel — DM only, never a public reply. Never discuss an individual's order in public comments.

2Messenger — instant reply, away message, FAQs

Same Automations screen · ~15 minutes

2a. Instant reply

Fires on the first message from anyone who has never messaged you. Its job is to buy you time and set an expectation — not to sell.

2b. Away message

Your stated hours are Mon–Sat, 12 PM – 10 PM PKT (that is what your shipping policy tells customers, so keep it consistent). Set the away schedule to everything outside that, including all day Sunday.

2c. FAQs

These show as tappable buttons when someone opens your chat. Meta usually allows four — use the four in the appendix, in that order. They are ranked by how often they block a sale.

3Instagram — the same three

~15 minutes

Instagram automations live in the same Business Suite Automations screen — when you create each one, tick Instagram as well as Messenger. Some settings only exist in the Instagram app itself.

3a. In Business Suite

Re-run Phase 1 and Phase 2 with the Instagram channel ticked. Most automations let you select both channels at once — if so, you may have already done this. Open each automation you built and confirm Instagram is on.

3b. In the Instagram app

Instagram app → your profile → ☰ → Business tools and controls:

  • Saved replies — the shortcuts from Phase 4
  • Frequently asked questions — same four
  • Welcome message — the instant reply
Story replies are your warmest lead Someone replying to a Story is further down the funnel than any comment. Do not let those sit overnight — the away message covers them, but check Story replies first every morning.

5Test it, then watch it for a week

15 minutes today, then five minutes a day

Test from a phone that is not an admin

  1. Comment "price?" on a recent Reel from a personal account. You should get a public reply and a DM within about a minute.
  2. Message the Page from that account — instant reply should fire, FAQ buttons should appear.
  3. Message at 11 PM — away message should fire instead.
  4. Comment "where is my order" — you should get a DM and no public reply.
Read every auto-reply that goes out for the first three days Keyword matching is blunt. "How much for the jacket" and "how much delivery" both contain the price trigger and will get the same answer. Watch for the mismatches and add the missing keyword to the right automation. After three days it settles.

What to tell me after a week

  • Roughly what share of messages you still answer by hand
  • The questions the automations keep getting wrong
  • The questions that come up a lot and have no automation yet

That list is exactly what Layer 2 gets built around — so it is trained on your real inbox rather than my guesses.
