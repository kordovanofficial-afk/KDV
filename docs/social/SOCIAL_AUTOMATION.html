Part 4 · Test it (Task 14)
14

Test from a phone that is not an admin

15 minutes · use a personal account, not the business one
It must not be an admin account Meta does not fire automations for people who manage the Page. Testing from your own admin account shows you nothing.
  1. Comment price? on a recent Reel → expect a public reply and a DM within a minute
  2. Comment where is my order → expect a DM and no public reply
  3. Comment asli hai? → expect the leather answer, not the price one
  4. Message the Page → instant reply fires, 4 FAQ buttons appear
  5. Message it after 10 PM → away message fires instead
  6. Repeat steps 1 and 4 on Instagram
Then read every auto-reply for three days Keyword matching is blunt. "How much for the jacket" and "how much delivery" both contain price words and will get the same answer. Watch for the mismatches and move the keyword to the right automation. It settles after about three days.
Complaints will trigger the sales pitch A comment like "price bohat zyada hai" or "fraud hai ye" contains your trigger words. If any automation screen offers an excluded keywords field, paste this list into it. If it does not, this is the main thing to watch in those first three days.
