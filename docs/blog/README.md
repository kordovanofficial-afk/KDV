# Blog article sources

Paste-ready article bodies for the Shopify blog. Each `.html` file is the **body**
only — schema block included at the top, no `<html>`/`<head>` wrapper. Paste the
whole file into the Shopify article editor in HTML mode.

Metadata for each article lives here rather than in the file, so nothing internal
ends up in the published page source.

### Why this article

GSC, 90 days to 18 Aug 2026 — every one of these is currently served by
`/collections/mens-leather-jackets`, which cannot answer a price question:

| Query | Impressions | Position | CTR |

| leather jackets in pakistan with price | 43 | 8.4 | 4.7% |

For contrast, `leather wallet price in pakistan` sits at position 5.6 — a tenth
of a position worse than the jacket query — and takes 5.9% of its impressions.
The gap is not ranking.

### Hub and spoke — do this or the two pages compete

`top-10-leather-jacket-brands-in-pakistan` is the hub and already holds position
2.1 on its head term. It contains a price-band section that overlaps this
article. **Trim that section to a three-line summary and link here**, so the
brands article keeps "brands" and this one takes "price". Do not leave both
pages arguing the same case at length.

The same article already covers the four leather-authenticity tests well. A
separate "real or fake" article would cannibalise it — expand that section in
place instead.

### Facts used, and where they came from

- Price bands (under Rs 8,000 / 8–15k / 20–40k / above 45k) — carried over from
  the brands article so the two agree. Not independently re-verified.
- Kordovan range Rs 22,000–35,000 across 30 active jackets — live catalogue,
  19 Aug 2026. Band counts: 6 / 12 / 5 / 7. **Re-check before publishing.**
- All jackets are top-grain sheep leather; cow to order, quoted per jacket via
  WhatsApp — user-supplied.
- Every size priced the same S–XXXL — verified from variant data.
- Cost per wear (Rs 135) is arithmetic on stated assumptions, and the assumptions
  are stated in the copy rather than hidden.

Nothing here claims a craft process for a jacket that does not have one, per the
claims rule in `ops/ADS_PLAYBOOK_PK.md` §7.
