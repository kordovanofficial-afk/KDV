2 · The office shoot (one session, about 40 min)

Before you press record

  • Phone: vertical, 4K or 1080p at 30 fps, back camera. Wipe the lens. Turn on the grid. Tap and hold on the product to lock focus and exposure (AE/AF lock).
  • Light: a window to the side, not behind you. Overhead lights off if they turn things yellow. Nothing harsh or flashy.
  • Desk: clear everything except the product. The best surfaces are a plain wooden desk, a sheet of brown kraft paper or a dark cloth. No logos from other brands, no papers with writing, no screens showing anything.
  • Hands: clean, sleeves rolled or a plain dark cuff. A watch is fine. No talking needed.
  • Every shot: hold still for 2 seconds before and after the action, and do each action 3 times. Before each one, say the shot number out loud ("shot four"); I use that to sort clips and mute it in the edit.
  • Phone stand: a tripod or a stack of books for the top-down shots. Handheld is fine for the slow pans.
